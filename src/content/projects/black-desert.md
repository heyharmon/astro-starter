sections:
  - heading: Precision laser-cut for fit-up
    body: >-
      While the railings appear to be traditional cable rail, the posts were
      laser-cut locally by Flog Industries for precision-aligned holes. This
      innovation streamlined production, ensured flawless fit-up, and delivered a
      level of quality expected in a luxury setting. Working with other top-tier
      contractors with the right tooling locally sets us up for success without
      having to outsource out of the region.
  - heading: Completed ahead of schedule
    body: >-
      Our team also integrated stainless-steel cable infills and lighted grab
      rails, bringing a sleek, modern finish that complements the resort's
      upscale design. Despite the tight tolerances and architectural demands, the
      work was completed ahead of schedule.
  - heading: We thrive in retrofits and love the challenges they present.
    body: >-
      Beyond the railings, we handled the structural steel inside the elevator
      shafts and miscellaneous metals throughout the resort. With no access from
      above, our crew relied on precision rigging and custom tooling to safely
      hoist and set the heavy components in place, another example of our
      problem-solving in complex environments.
