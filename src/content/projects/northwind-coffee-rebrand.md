## The Challenge

After eight years as a single neighborhood cafe, the team was launching a wholesale program and needed a brand that could stand on a grocery shelf next to national roasters. The existing logo and packaging worked in the cafe but did not translate to a sales sheet, a pallet, or a competitive website.

## The Approach

We rebuilt the visual identity from the ground up — a refined wordmark, a tighter color palette, and packaging that held up at retail scale. Alongside the brand work, we shipped a new ecommerce site with subscriptions, wholesale ordering, and a story-driven roasting page that gave buyers a reason to choose them.

## The Outcome

Online subscriptions grew steadily through the first year, the wholesale program signed its first regional grocery account within two months of launch, and the team now has a brand system that flexes from a coffee bag to a delivery truck without losing its character.
