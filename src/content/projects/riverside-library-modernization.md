## The Challenge

The library system was running on a decade-old website that struggled to serve a growing community. Patrons could not easily find branch hours, reserve materials, or sign up for events without bouncing between three disconnected tools. Staff were fielding the same questions by phone every day.

## The Approach

We started by interviewing librarians and patrons to map the most common tasks. From there, we rebuilt the site around a clear information architecture, integrated the catalog directly into search, and replaced the third-party event tool with a calendar that staff could update without help from IT.

## The Outcome

In the first three months after launch, online room reservations doubled, support calls about hours and locations dropped by roughly forty percent, and the events calendar became the most-visited section of the site. Staff now manage all day-to-day content themselves.
