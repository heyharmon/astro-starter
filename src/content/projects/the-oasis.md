This 6,000-square-foot restaurant and amenity station is situated in the heart of the Black Desert Golf Course, between holes 10, 11, 15, and 16, and will host a PGA FedEx Cup event in October 2024. This unique design features a rooftop dining area with stunning views of the surrounding golf course, lava flows, canyons, and mountains. A signature element of the property, the building incorporates stone cladding harvested from the site's basalt flows.
